# Private rotating EXTRNL entry epochs; independent WAD-input experiment

Generate `entry.py --source PARENT/source --out NEW_DIR --variant entry` and apply the two emitted files only in an isolated build. It keeps the existing nine-field bidirectional local-boundary path for public/nonrotating or nonadmitted cases, and the existing full-gather path for unproved boundary graphs. The optimization requires `ROTATE`, `SMPI_LOCALBC`, the full `SMPI_OUTER` admission, and no sparse Q/EPC/diffuser lists. Validation of sparse boundary and corner copies belongs to `SECOM_MPI_BC_PLAN` at startup; actual corner destinations need not be away from a seam because EXTRNL retains its post-corner UA/VA refresh before momentum. A team barrier precedes the first read of `IEXT` (published by MASTER without an exit barrier).

| EXTRNL entry | Receives from south | Receives from north |
|---|---|---|
| First substep, every outer step | D, UA, UAB, EL, ELB, DUMWAD | D, VA, VAB |
| Later substeps of same outer step | UA, UAB, ELB, DUMWAD | VAB |

The preceding private WAD has already supplied bilateral D, EL before its two colors (neither is written inside WAD), and bilateral VA/DVMWAD on return. CHECK does not change them. EXTRNL needs D on both sides, EL to the south, VA to the north. `ROTATE` assigns UAB, VAB and ELB each substep; do not reuse these old states. WAD updates horizontal DUMWAD (no final halo) and UA. ADVAVE reads DVMWAD only at `(I,J)` and `(I-1,J)`; its north-row WAD consumer gets a fresh entry halo inside WAD. No implicit arithmetic, sign conversion, field masking or MPI reduction is introduced in this transport. The runtime uses two distinct directional `MPI_Sendrecv`s with counts 3+6 planes first, 1+4 thereafter, instead of nine bilateral halos (18 calls, 18 field rows). Allocated workspace is released in `SMPI_FINALIZE`; packed-count overflow uses per-field traffic in the same direction.

`--variant wadinput` is a **separate** experiment. It retains the old EXTRNL full DVMWAD entry publication and omits WAD's immediately following duplicate DVMWAD entry halo only for the known private EL,D call. It preserves the public WAD contract and its post-color publication. Do not combine `entry` with `wadinput`: the first substep's outer mask reset would leave both send sites absent. The generators reject this layering; do not merge them manually. A future north-only WAD variant must preserve not just D± but also the south EL reuse promise (or prove/copy the already published ELF bits).

## Evidence

`p3-entry-r009a.json` and `.log` are from GNU 8.5/OpenMPI 4.1.1 on explicitly reserved CPU24–31; no full solver was started. Each mode uses the real EXTRNL and ADVAVE, real WAD and CHECK, and the two extracted BCOND list loops for IDX 12/13; other boundary types are hard-failing stubs. The 372×19 fixture runs 2 outer steps × 4 substeps (nonlinear then linear advection), dynamic dry masks, a checked-read local corner on a seam, and a nonempty boundary list. The initial/reused states are re-poisoned each step except for the precise previous-state sides required. Its 24 owner-plane captures and full extremum bits/coordinates compare against exact slices of an independently run **single-rank** kernel with the same corner geometry. 3×1 validates active, LOCALBC=false, and OUTER=false; 2×2 validates the active path. Deliberate missing south EL (step 1), stale north VAB (step 2) and stale WAD DVMWAD all change owned outputs and are detected. Max present message sizes: 8,928 bytes first, 5,952 bytes later. No complete solver speedup is inferred from the fixture.

`test_entry.py stage NEW/<old|entry|wadinput|bad-next-vab|bad-first-el|bad-wad-mask> --source PARENT/source --role ROLE` prepares each isolated source tree. `native_entry.py NEW --cpus CPU_LIST` runs them, bound to distinct previously allocated physical cores within the campaign. The fixture sources must be generated from their parent, not from a tree that already has either entry modification applied. `flux_direction.py` is independent (see `FLUX_DIRECTION.md`). For combination, generate the `entry` overlay, then generate the `flux_direction` overlay against that complete temporary parent tree or apply in the reverse order; do not overwrite a previously modified `extrnl.f` with an overlay generated from an earlier source.
