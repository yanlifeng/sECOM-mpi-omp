r004b / BAROTROPIC outer-state publication review (2026-11-19)

Scope: cold start, MPI>1, SMPI_LOCALBC and SMPI_OUTER admitted before the
time loop. Require INTERNAL, CLAMPED, CONSTANT mixing, MANNINGS-N,
AVERAGED/no-PET, non-time-varying ice, JTM=0, ISPLIT>0, no tracer,
sediment, chemistry or particles, no stations. Do not generalize to
arbitrary input decks or mixed/3-D branches. Line labels below refer
to source/ecom3d.f. No additional non-owner reader found in the gated
main path after removal of the 24 per-outer-step bulk publications.

Before/inside the subcycle. Startup readers, FIRST, and startup ARCHIVE
run while fields are replicated. On each outer step only BCOND(7)'s
three dense forcing passes are owned; all scalar/record reads and the
ordered boundary lists are unchanged. The V-momentum stencil requires
the south TYSURF and CBC after their producers; BOTTAU's VBAR requires
north VA. Each has its explicit halo. Rain/evaporation, T/S updates,
DT used in Manning, UBAR and QBAR otherwise read only the owner row.
Constant mixing skips SMAG and BAROTROPIC skips the 3-D pressure and
flux preparation. The subcycle's staged halos, WAD and replicated
extrema verdict are unchanged; VAMAX>100 branches on every rank.

Step 1 is different. Label 8001 branches to 8200, bypassing archive.
DO 380 must stay JMAINLO:JMAINHI: EGB=EGF, ETB=ET, ET=ETF,
DT=H+ET, UTB=UTF, VTB=VTF are all same-row reads/writes. NUMEPC=0.
This preserves owner DT for next BCOND(7) and Manning. No peer reads
of these six updated fields occur before the next event/halo.

Steps 2 onward. After 8001, the history zero certificate scans owned
rows and reduces the veto. SECOM_ARCHIVE_BODY updates owned rows;
its own EL/VA halos and event publications handle cross-row energy
and history consumers. It returns to 9900, NOT 8200. At 9900 the
tracer, particle and sediment/chemistry blocks are skipped by the
gate, as is EXTERNAL reconstruction. TOR=BAROTROPIC then jumps to
9910, bypassing 3-D momentum, velocity clamping, WREAL and DO 380.
There is no extra dense physics reader on this 9900 -> 9910 path.

External consumers. All ranks participate in SMPI_OUTER_SNAPSHOT
before scheduled PRINTS, the in-loop restart, the end-of-run restart,
and at 9100 for diagnostics (also reached on abort). Root-only file
writes do not predicate the collective. The frozen startup record
needs D/DT, CURV42D/TXBOT/TYBOT, UA/UAB/VA/VAB, EL/ELB/ETF/ET/ETB,
ADVUA/ADVVA, TXICE/TYICE, DUMWAD/DVMWAD, first-step EGB/UTB/VTB and
possibly rain-modified T/TB/S/SB. Other record fields are unchanged
in this admitted path. CBC, UBAR/VBAR, QBAR and TAU are not in the
frozen restart layout; snapshots still cover them for diagnostics.
BCOND's saved local scratch PRECTEMP is not COMMON and must never be
published as a grid. Startup, history output and PRINTS have separate
contracts: a full restart snapshot is not a substitute for a history
event publication.
